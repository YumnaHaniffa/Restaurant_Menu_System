OUR STORY

A Place Where
Luxsury Meets Tradition

At To Vang, we bring you the authentic taste of Vietnam with a unique twist. The name “Towda” means “stone bowl” in Vietnamese, reflecting our signature method of serving hot, flavorful pho in traditional stone bowls to preserve its rich taste and warmth

Inspired by the vibrant flavors and culture of Vietnam, we’ve crafted a menu that combines timeless recipes with a modern touch. From our hearty pho soup to freshly made banh mi and appetizers, every dish is prepared with love, fresh ingredients, and a passion for excellence.

Whether you’re here for comfort food, a family meal, or an adventurous dining experience, Pho Towda is where flavor meets tradition

Join us and savor the essence of Vietnam- one bowl at a time!
